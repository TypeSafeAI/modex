## Budgets (#327 AC5)

Median of five launches, from the click to the content on screen, through the production read service with a synthetic
`gh`. Times are on a Mac17,6 (Apple M5 Max) under other sessions' load, about 7. "Delayed" slows each `gh` answer by 5.5 s
through `MODEX_BUDGET_GH_DELAY_MS`, the failure proof.

| Path | Run 1 (ms) | Run 2 (ms) | Budget (ms) | Delayed (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open the inbox | 821 | 317 | 5,000 | 17,406, fails |
| Open an overview | 97 | 98 | 1,500 | 5,842, fails |
| Open a 1,000-file diff (first page) | 219 | 224 | 2,500 | 22,486, fails |
| Open a 3 MiB patch | 308 | 307 | 3,000 | 33,560, fails |

Budgets leave roughly tenfold headroom. The 500 ms transcript gate failed on hosted runners from variance alone, so
these catch a change several times slower, and no longer. The inbox is bimodal here, about 310 or 820 ms; the cause was
not investigated. **Not yet seen on a hosted runner.** The synthetic `gh` answers at once, so none of this measures GitHub.

## Limits

- **Attachments during a turn.** Attaching is refused while a turn runs, by design. The CF-3 test therefore attaches while
  idle and starts the waiting turn through `thread:send`, as a turn from another client would.
- **The hard-kill path.** The layout saves through localStorage, so a crash immediately after the toggle can lose it, as
  with every layout preference. The rollback test uses a normal quit.
- **Gates still open.** Live CR-6 smoke, live CR-7 canary, VoiceOver, keyboard A rows, CF-3 D and P rows and the reference
  decision. All are in [the Val session](2026-11-02-v0.5.0-val-session.md). This candidate runs the default-on step ahead of
  them, which is [decision 1.1](2026-11-02-v0.5.0-val-session.md#1-decisions-no-launch-10-minutes) there.
