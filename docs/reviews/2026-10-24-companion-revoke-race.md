The new regression passes against existing production code. A deliberate mutation only in generated JavaScript captured the token when a listener started instead of reading the current token for authorization. The regression then failed at the old-token request with `200 !== 401`. The original generated bytes were restored immediately; no production mutation is included. Focused companion/network tests passed 27/27 with zero skips, including the real LAN cases. This is mutation sensitivity evidence, not a newly discovered production defect.

The existing listener and lifecycle test infrastructure comes from #166. #109 credits @Yash990-bit's #124 regression work and identifies the limitations of its rejected wildcard-binding approach; this increment does not reintroduce wildcard binding or claim a new network architecture.

## Physical acceptance still needed

Use a Mac connected to two different private LAN subnets, such as Ethernet and Wi-Fi, and a physical iPhone on the secondary subnet. Record macOS, Modex version/commit, Companion version/build, interface roles and sanitized subnet labels. Do not publish pairing tokens, QR payloads, certificate private keys or full diagnostics containing credentials.

1. Enable Companion, select the secondary Mac network, scan its QR code, and confirm the phone loads projects and a thread over the secondary endpoint with certificate pinning intact.
2. Relaunch the paired phone app and verify credential-free Bonjour discovery reconnects through the reachable secondary endpoint when the primary endpoint is unreachable from the phone.
3. Change the primary address or disconnect that interface while keeping the secondary LAN available. Verify the secondary connection continues and its pairing remains valid.
4. Change the secondary address through DHCP, then confirm saved pairing recovers through discovery without re-pairing or accepting a different certificate.
5. During recovery, choose Forget paired phones. Confirm the previous pairing can no longer read or act, and a newly scanned pairing succeeds. Repeat with Turn off and confirm no endpoint remains available until explicitly enabled.
