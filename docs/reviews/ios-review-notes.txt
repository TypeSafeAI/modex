Review access: Modex Companion 0.1.0, build 5

On a fresh installation, tap "Explore a demo workspace" below "Pair with your Mac". This opens a built-in offline workspace. No account, Mac, network connection, camera permission, subscription or expiring pairing code is required.

Optional demo QR: attach modex-demo-qr.png from this review packet. It encodes exactly modex://demo and contains no credentials. Open it with iPhone Camera, or scan it in the app's pairing sheet. Opening modex://demo directly reaches the same demo. The welcome-screen button is the fallback when reviewing on a single device.

QR image: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TypeSafeAI/modex/main/docs/reviews/modex-demo-qr.png

Suggested review steps:
1. Open "Review launch changes". Tap "Approve once", confirm, and observe the approval result. Approvals only change the demo transcript.
2. Enter a follow-up and tap the send arrow. A clearly scripted response appears.
3. Return to the thread list and inspect the other sample threads. Immediately after opening or resetting the demo, the release-notes thread briefly shows Running before completing.
4. Open Workspace options (the ellipsis) and choose "Reset demo workspace" to restore the pending approval. You can then test "Deny".
5. "Leave demo workspace" returns to welcome. "Pair with your Mac..." remains available. Quitting the app discards the demo.

The demo is visibly labeled and available to every user. Its provider names, tool activity and responses are examples; no AI request or command runs. Real operation uses a private, authenticated connection to the user's Mac running Modex. The Mac keeps coding execution and Claude/Codex accounts. A real pairing QR is private to that Mac and network, so it cannot serve as reusable remote review access.

We request acceptance of this built-in demo as review access under Guideline 2.1. Please let us know if you require an additional live-host review arrangement. Apple approval of demo access and the app remains pending.
