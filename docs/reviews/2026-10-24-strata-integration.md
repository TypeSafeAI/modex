Explicit desktop local selection uses LM-1 schema-1 identity and LM-2's encrypted credentials/revision binding. Main resolves the revision before every turn and again after queue admission. Native Codex children use private home/configuration directories, connection-scoped credentials, an environment allowlist and exact model/provider selection. Ordinary native event/approval/Stop handling is reused.

Existing project `.codex` directories and hostname-only endpoints are refused pending native configuration/DNS qualification. This is a bounded pilot, not completion of every acceptance criterion in #379–#383 or a universal/offline compatibility claim.
