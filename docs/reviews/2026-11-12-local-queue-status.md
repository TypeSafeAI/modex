# Local-service queue progress and cancellation

LM-5 (#380) requires explicit status while one Modex turn waits for another turn on the same local service. The delivered semaphore serialized turns but supplied no waiting notice. A blocked person could see a running turn without knowing that native execution had not started.

The repair emits one fixed host notice when a turn first waits for capacity, then a neutral continuation notice after acquisition and connection/disposal checks. Repeated wakeups do not repeat the waiting notice. Both messages use the existing redacting sink, durable transcript and renderer notice path. They contain no profile, model, endpoint or credential input.

Cancellation is checked immediately after acquiring the slot, with its release callback already assigned. This closes the handoff where cancellation can arrive after acquisition's final check but before its caller resumes. A cancelled turn releases its slot without announcing continuation or opening a native child. The continuation text does not claim that every native process from the preceding turn has terminated.
